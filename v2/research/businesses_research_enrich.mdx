Run a custom, AI-powered research task over a list of businesses.

<Info>
  **Beta.** Request/response shape and the credit model may change before GA. During the beta, research runs in sync mode only.
</Info>

This endpoint accepts either a single ID (a string) or a list of IDs — see [v2 conventions](/v2/conventions).
