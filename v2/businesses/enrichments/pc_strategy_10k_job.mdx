<Note>
  **Asynchronous variant.** This submits a job through the [async job infrastructure](/v2/async-jobs) — poll [job status](/v2/jobs/get_job_status) for progress and results. Limits: max 10,000 input records, max 24-hour run time.

  For the full output signal reference, see [the synchronous endpoint](/v2/businesses/enrichments/pc_strategy_10k_enrich).
</Note>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How It Works">
    - **Input:** Provide a list of up to 10,000 `business_ids`.
    - **Processing:** The system retrieves strategic insights for all submitted businesses in a **single request**.
    - **Output:** A structured response with corporate focus, partnerships, and competitive strategies.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Example Request (cURL)">
    ```shell Bash
    curl --request POST \
         --url https://api.explorium.ai/v2/businesses/pc_strategy_10k/job \
         --header 'accept: application/json' \
         --header 'api_key: your_api_key_here' \
         --header 'content-type: application/json' \
         --data '{
      "business_ids": [
        "8adce3ca1cef0c986b22310e369a0793",
        "a34bacf839b923770b2c360eefa26748"
      ]
    }'
    ```
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Best Practices">
    - **Assess corporate strategies** to understand business priorities.
    - **Identify partnerships and alliances** to track business relationships.
    - **Leverage strategic insights** for competitive benchmarking.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
