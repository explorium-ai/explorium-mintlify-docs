---
title: "Create business enrollment"
sidebarTitle: "Create enrollment"
openapi: "post /v2/businesses/events/enrollments"
---

Requires a `webhook_id` of an **active** [v2 webhook](/v2/endpoints/webhooks) in your scope — unknown IDs return `404`, a missing `webhook_id` or wrong scope/status returns `422`; delivery is never silently re-pointed. Up to **20 IDs per request**, unlimited total across requests.
